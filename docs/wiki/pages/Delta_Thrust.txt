==Notes==
*[[Plague]] effect is -6MP/[[tick]] and -100TP/[[tick]].
*As with all Blue Magic additional effects, [[Plague]] does not always land on the target. This is especially true with Notorious and high level monsters.
**[[Magic Accuracy]] and [[Blue Magic Skill]] increase the odds of landing the additional effect.
* Bug: In the English localization, the spell description is incorrect. As of June 2022, the spell description says "Delivers a threefold attack. Additional effect: Plague. '''Chance of effect''' varies with TP.". 
** It should read "Delivers a threefold attack. Additional effect: Plague. '''Effect duration''' varies with TP." (difference bolded). The help text on the quick menu is correct.
