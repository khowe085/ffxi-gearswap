'''Nolan''', located in [[Norg]] at (I-7); offers the service of upgrading certain equipment and weapons obtained from [[Escha]], a process called "mezzotinting". The armor and weapons are gradually upgraded through fifteen ranks with increasing and expanding stats by applying [[Eschalixir|Eschalixirs]]. The initial cost of starting the augment process is 100 [[Escha Silt]]. Thereafter, the cost is 1 Silt per level.

Augment paths may be switched without losing current rank progression, at a cost of 3,000 Silt.

* Nolan will exchange [[Escha Bead|Escha Beads]] for [[Eschalixir|Eschalixirs]] for  at the following rate:
**1 [[File:Eschalixir icon.png|25px|link=]] [[Eschalixir]] = 10 Beads
**1 [[File:Eschalixir +1 icon.png|25px|link=]] [[Eschalixir +1]] = 50 Beads
**1 [[File:Eschalixir +2 icon.png|25px|link=]] [[Eschalixir +2]] = 2,000 Beads

* [[Zurim]] sells 1 [[File:Eschalixir +2 icon.png|25px|link=]] [[Eschalixir +2]] for 10 [[Domain Points]].

* There are four upgrade paths for each armor and weapon to choose from. Paths A, B, and C are the same for each set of armor, with Path D being different between the head/body/hands/legs/feet.

* Escha gear requires '''5,980''' RP to upgrade fully to Rank 15.
** 1 [[File:Eschalixir icon.png|25px|link=]] [[Eschalixir]] = 10 RP
** 1 [[File:Eschalixir +1 icon.png|25px|link=]] [[Eschalixir +1]] = 50 RP
** 1 [[File:Eschalixir +2 icon.png|25px|link=]] [[Eschalixir +2]] = 2,000 RP

To begin the process, trade the item by itself to Nolan and select the desired augment path.  You will be charged 100 silt (to activate the first path) or 3000 silt (to change paths) upon trading.  Next, trade the item and the desired number of eschalixirs at the same time to Nolan in order to add rank points to the item; each trade costs 1 silt.

Augment progress may be checked by looking at the item's info box in the inventory or equipment screen.  An extra line will be added which looks like so:

<Type:X/Rank:Y/RP:Z>

Where X is the augment path chosen (A-D), Y is the current strength of the augment (Rank 1 - Rank 15), and Z is the number of rank points required for the next rank.  The number decreases as more eschalixirs are traded; when the number hits 0, a rank is gained on the item.

Equipment that may be augmented and their max rank augments are found below.

{{color|red|Note!}} All of the equipment below has the "AugSendable" tag. This means that they can be sent to alternate characters on the same PlayOnline account, even after they are augmented!
