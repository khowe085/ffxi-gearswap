==Notes==
*Unlike [[Self-Destruct]] this spell does not inflict [[weakness]] to the caster.
*Damage is proportional to the HP of the caster.
*Damage potential caps on a particular target if your current HP > 1/2 target's max HP. 
**The lower the mob level the higher the cap damage:
***Max of 65% of monster's HP in damage to anything that checks "Too weak to be worthwhile" to weaker "Decent challenge" foes. 
***Max of 50% of monster's HP in damage on harder "Decent challenge" to "Even match" foes. 
*Possesses properties of both physical and magical blue magic. You may skillchain with [[Chain Affinity]], but resist rates on damage are light based. {{Information Needed}}
*Melee accuracy has nothing to do with the spell landing. {{Information Needed}}
*This spell ignores Utsusemi  [[Shadow Image]]s.
*If the spell misses ("fails to activate") the user will lose no HP.
