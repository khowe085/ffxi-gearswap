==Notes==
* May only be cast with the ability [[Unbridled Learning]].
* Approximately -33% [[Defense Down]].
**[[Magic Accuracy]] and [[Blue Magic Skill]] increase the chances of the defense down taking effect.
*[[Defense Down]] effects do not stack with each other. 
**[[Dia]], [[Box Step]], [[Defense Down]], and [[Geo-Frailty]] all stack together.
**[[Defense Down]] effects such as [[Acid Bolt]]s, [[Angon (Ability)|Angon]], [[Spirit Surge]]d Jumps, [[Tachi: Ageha]], [[Full Break]], [[Shell Crusher]], [[Tenebral Crush]], [[Sweeping Gouge]], [[Tourbillion]], and [[Frightful Roar]], etc do not stack. Only one may be active at a time.
